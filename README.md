**Project:** Kisan Mitra (Farmer’s Friend AI Plant Disease Detector)

**Theme:** Science, Technology, and Innovation for Sustainable and Viksit Bharat (Category C1)

* **What it is:** A free web app that works on any smartphone browser without installing software.
* **How it works:**
1. The farmer points their phone camera at an infected leaf.
2. The app captures the image and sends it to a cloud vision AI model.
3. Within seconds, it displays the plant name, disease diagnosis, and treatment remedies (both organic and chemical).


* **Key Benefit:** Gives farmers early detection of crop blights directly in the field, preventing harvest loss and reducing unnecessary pesticide use.
